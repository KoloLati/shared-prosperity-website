Episodes

One episode, two formats — for now.

Every episode currently airing on PTCV pairs an 8–12 minute host-led Brief Talk with a 35–45 minute Conversation with a local leader, recorded and released together as a single episode.

Why one format right now: Season 1 was produced and is airing as combined episodes, so Brief Talks and Conversations aren't separable yet. Each card below shows both halves of the same episode. Starting Season 2, once Brief Talks and Conversations are recorded on independent schedules, they'll split into their own sections and nav tabs.

Release cadence: new episodes release roughly twice a month through November 2026. This list grows one card at a time as each episode airs.

Our Founding Story

Can a single decision made in a small city change the world?

At twelve, our founder fled civil war in his town and spent over a year in the forest. Years later, he arrived in the United States as an international student. Pittsfield, Massachusetts became home — and that place shaped this venture.

Prosperity does not begin everywhere at once. It begins somewhere — with the choices we make.

America's 250th

Celebrate America's 250th with a Value-Driven Mission.

As our nation approaches its 250 years, the true enduring vision of America remains rooted in the promise that equal opportunity and collective progress are created, not given. Shared Prosperity honors this legacy by proving that the future of American economic resilience is still written at the local level. By transforming the decisions of our small-city leaders into actionable blueprints, we are ensuring that the next chapter of the American story is one where local opportunity still shapes global wealth — proving that when our hometowns thrive, their ideas, skills, and innovations lift communities across the country and beyond.

Our Impact

Through the support of our partners and collaborators, Shared Prosperity transforms small-city leadership decisions into public understanding and actionable civic insights that benefit our own community, regions across the country, and beyond.

Community engagement

Corps of Volunteers

Direct community service — needs at understood scale, awareness, and engagement, having anyone.

Convening · 2027

The Forum

Annual Pittsfield convening with cross-community learning, civic stakeholders, and public sector leaders.

Talent pipeline · 2027

The Fellowship

An 8–12 week fellowship for high school/college students, featured in part in honor of Dr. Latifah Phillips.

Recognition · 2028

Prize for Resilience

An annual award for a small city that has shown its ability to adapt and embrace adversity as a catalyst for entrepreneurial creativity.

Other Programs

Ideas being evaluated, not yet committed — listed here alongside Corps of Volunteers, The Forum, The Fellowship, and Prize for Resilience as the fuller program roadmap.

Format R&D · Candidate

Civic Media Innovation Lab

An internal R&D function that tests new civic media formats — documentary video, audio-first segments, illustrated long-form, data dashboards — and publishes what works.

Cross-Continental · Candidate

Diaspora Bridges Initiative

Connects US small-city leadership with local leadership in small communities across sub-Saharan Africa through co-productions, exchanges, and shared archives.

Practice Incubator · Candidate

Replication Lab

A light-touch incubator helping communities replicate a playbook from a Shared Prosperity episode, with consulting, peer connections, and a documented record.

Flagship Publication · Candidate

Annual State of Small Cities Report

An annual report synthesizing the season's coverage into the state of decision-making in American small cities — a citable reference for funders and civic leaders.

Education · Candidate

Shared Prosperity Schools Curriculum

An adapted version of the venture's storytelling model for high schools and community colleges, with lesson plans and student-production guidance.

Mentorship · Candidate

Storytellers Apprenticeship Program

A structured mentorship pairing students and early-career creators with Shared Prosperity's team for hands-on civic media training.
